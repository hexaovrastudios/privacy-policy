Hexa 2048: Cosmic Voxel Saga

Official Privacy Policy & Account Data Deletion

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

Hexaovra Studios ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of players enjoying Hexa 2048: Cosmic Voxel Saga. This single comprehensive policy details our data collection, security measures, third-party integrations, and account deletion procedures.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide core 2048 gameplay, cloud save functionality, and ad-supported monetization:

  • Account Credentials (Optional): When signing in via Google Sign-In, we collect your display name, email address, and unique user ID (UID).
  • Game Progress & Cloud Save Data: Your earned Coins, Gems, unlocked 3D Voxel Skins, and 1,000 Campaign Level completion records are securely backed up to Firebase Firestore.
  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostics: Google AdMob collects advertising IDs, device model information, and crash diagnostics for non-intrusive ad delivery and frequency capping.

2. Third-Party Integrations

Our app incorporates trusted Google SDKs built in compliance with global privacy regulations:

  • Google Play Services & Billing: Powers app distribution and secure in-app purchases.
  • Google AdMob: Delivers banner, rewarded video, and 2-minute frequency cap interstitial ads. Google Ad Terms.
  • Firebase Auth & Firestore: Manages secure user authentication and cloud game progress database. Firebase Privacy Terms.

3. Data Encryption & Security

All data exchanged between your mobile device and our cloud servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS 256-bit SSL encryption. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Hexa 2048: Cosmic Voxel Saga is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Account & Data Deletion Instructions

You have full ownership of your data and can request permanent deletion of your account and cloud save records at any time through either of the following methods:

Method A: Instant In-App Account Deletion

  1. Open Hexa 2048: Cosmic Voxel Saga on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Account / Profile button on the Main Hub screen.
  3. Tap "Delete Account & Wipe Cloud Data".
  4. Confirm your selection. Your account, profile info, and cloud database records will be erased instantly.

Method B: Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have the game installed, send an email request to our support team:
• Email: hexaovrastudios@gmail.com
• Subject Line: Account Deletion Request - Hexa 2048
• Details: Mention your registered Google email or UID. Requests are fulfilled within 24–48 hours.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All earned Coins, Gems, unlocked Voxel Skins, and level progress will be permanently wiped.
